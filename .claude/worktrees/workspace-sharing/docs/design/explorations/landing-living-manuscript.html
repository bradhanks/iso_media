§ 01 — The panel
I

The methodologist

Reads for rigor. Pushes on every claim that outruns its evidence and every result the data can't quite carry.

Overstated findings Missing controls & caveats Statistical hand-waving
II

The editor

Reads for clarity. Untangles long sentences, kills hedging, and keeps your argument moving from line to line.

Buried topic sentences Passive, padded prose Undefined jargon
III

The librarian

Reads for the record. Checks that claims are cited, sources are real, and the literature you lean on actually says what you say.

Uncited assertions Misattributed quotes Gaps in related work
