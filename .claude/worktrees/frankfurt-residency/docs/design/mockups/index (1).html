About us

Built by a researcher, for researchers

DK
Deanna Kepka, PhD, MPH
Founder & Director
Professor, University of Utah College of Nursing
Investigator, Huntsman Cancer Institute

PerfectPaper was founded by Deanna Kepka, PhD, MPH — a professor at the University of Utah College of Nursing and an investigator at Huntsman Cancer Institute, where her research on HPV vaccination and population health has informed public-health practice for over a decade.

Over her career she has reviewed hundreds of manuscripts — for journals, grant panels, and the researchers she mentors — and kept seeing the same pattern: strong work held back by fixable problems no one caught in time. PerfectPaper is the distillation of that work. She set out to encode her reviewing expertise and rigor into a system that gives every paper the same careful, exacting read — at scale, across many manuscripts at once.
